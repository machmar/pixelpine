## What the numbers say

- **The LEDs drive everything.** At 1 000 pcs the LEDs are 86% of the BOM, and their 480 joints are 87% of the assembly bill. Halving the LED count would cut the unit cost by roughly a third — but 240 dense LEDs *are* the product; keep them.
- **The MCU is almost free.** The PY32F003F18P6 (64 KB flash) is ~$0.13 in reels, cheaper than the 16 KB CH32V003 it replaced; a charlieplex driver chip such as the IS31FL3731 on the reference board costs over 10× more.
- **At 100 pcs compliance kills the margin** — treat it as a pilot (samples, school fair, beta testers) and keep those units off the market until the EMC report exists. At 1 000 pcs the classic retail channel loses money because the €2 700 compliance budget is spread over too few units; own e-shop and B2B promo orders carry it. From ~3 000 pcs retail works, and at 10 000 pcs every channel is healthy.
- **Competing with AliExpress** (bare soldering kits at $2.5–4.3 shipped) on price alone is not possible at the retail shelf — the landed volume cost is close to their selling price, and EU compliance plus VAT is a cost those listings often skip. The product has to win on looks (dense warm-white matrix, animations, ready-built) and sit in the €10–15 gift slot. See [market-analysis.md](market-analysis.md).

## Cost-down levers not yet taken

| Lever | Saving / unit | Trade-off |
|---|---:|---|
| Direct LED reel quote instead of LCSC list | ≈ $0.40 | needs 250 k+ LEDs per order |
| Cold-white basic LED (C2290) | ≈ $0.05 + $3/order | loses the warm look |
| Drop C1 (10 µF) and rely on C2 + USB source capacitance | ≈ $0.02 | more supply ripple on touch sensing |
| Stand fin from the PCB panel's waste corners instead of its own area | ≈ $0.03 | tighter panel layout |
| Kraft paper sleeve instead of a printed box | ≈ $0.10 | less gift appeal |
